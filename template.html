Chris Fazzino's Virtual Portfolio

Hello! My name is Chris Fazzino and this is my virtual portfolio. I am a an artist and videographer and a computer sience major at CT State Community College specializing in general programming. I have been working on some web development projects, facebook automation, and a slew of projects in python. I have experience producing and editing films and have had a passion for photography since I was 12 years old. This website is meant to be a showcase for unpublished photograpy, coding projects, video production projects, and any other multimedia that fits into this subset of my work.
